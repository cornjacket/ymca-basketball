## 1-3-1 pattern — pass and screen away

**Spots** (tight — wings and posts only a step or two outside the lane):

```
              P               P  = point (top of the key)
      LW      H      RW       LW/RW = left / right wing (elbow extended)
                              H  = high post (free throw line)
              B               B  = baseline (short corner / under the rim)
```

**Rule:** after you pass, **screen away** from the ball for the nearest
teammate; the player you screen **cuts to your old spot** to be the next
receiver.

**No standing around — move in, move out.** Anyone not passing or screening
is always moving: **step in** toward the lane, then **pop back out** to their
spot to get open. Guards move in and out to **shake their defender** — a guard
who stands still gets the ball taken.

1. **P → LW.** P screens away for **RW**; RW pops to the point. P fills RW.
2. **LW → P** (new point). LW screens down for **B**; B pops out to LW. LW fills B.
3. **P → RW.** P screens away for the **new LW**, who pops to the point. P fills LW.
4. **RW → H.** RW screens down for **B**; B pops to RW. RW fills B.
5. **H → open shooter** — the new RW, or B (old RW) cutting to the rim. **Shoot.**

Run it to the right next time (start **P → RW**) so both sides get reps. Once
the pattern is automatic, let the passer choose any spot on pass 5.
