# Wednesday 10/14 — 60 min

Built on the 10/7 plan. The two **hot-potato 1-3-1** blocks are out. In their
place: **2-1-2 / 2-2 keep away** and a block to install **the play**. The
scrimmage is now **half court**, and its format depends on how many players show up.

| Time | Block | Detail | Court | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Roundtable | Circle up: **player names**, talk through the **last game**, and preview today's drills. | Sideline / No basket | Set the tone, build team identity |
| 5 min | 1v1 dribble battle | Pairs, **spaced well apart** from other pairs. Both players dribble and try to **knock the opponent's ball away** while protecting their own. **If your ball gets knocked out, you lose.** Run several short rounds. **After each round, winners pair up with winners and losers with losers.** | Spread out / No basket | Protect the ball with body and off arm, eyes up, quick hands |
| 10 min | Layup / rebound / outlet | Sideline to sideline, **counter-clockwise**. | Sideline to sideline | Finish, rebound, and outlet on the move |
| 10 min | 3-man weave + 2v1 | Run the weave, then go straight into a **2v1**. | Sideline to sideline | Turn the weave into an advantage finish; defend outnumbered |
| 10 min | 2-1-2 / 2-2 keep away | Offense sets up in a **2-1-2** (5 players) or a **2-2** (4 players). Defenders try to **steal or deflect** the ball. Offense **moves to get open** and **passes to keep it away**. If the defense gets a steal, the teams swap. | Half court | Get open, pass away from pressure, keep spacing |
| 10 min | The play | **TBD — coach to fill in.** Walk it through unguarded first, then run it against defense. | Half court | Run the play on command |
| 10 min | Scrimmage | **Half court.** **8 players → 4v4. 10 players → 5v5. 9 players → 3v3v3:** two teams play while the third waits at half court, and the waiting team rotates in on every score or stop. **Rule:** it's a **turnover if you're too close to the ball carrier**. | Half court | Spread out, move the ball, play the game |
