# Wednesday 10/21 — 60 min

Built on the 10/14 plan. This week adds the **screen and roll**, **1v1 man
coverage** (stay in front), and the **help defense shell**. A whiteboard talk on
**corners** sets up the half-court finish, where the high post screens and rolls.

| Time | Block | Detail | Court | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Roundtable | Circle up: **player names**, then talk through **last week's successes** and **what needs to improve**. Preview today's drills. | Sideline / No basket | Set the tone, build team identity |
| 10 min | Screen and roll | **As a team:** everyone learns to **roll** — pivot open toward the ball, show a target hand, and cut to the basket. **Then one by one:** each player **sets a screen** (wide, legal base, hold still) and **rolls to the basket** for a pass and layup. | Main basket | Learn the roll, then the screen-and-roll |
| 10 min | 1v1 man coverage | **Part 1 (main basket):** start at the **elbow**. **No scoring** — the defender slides **sideways and backwards** and keeps the opponent **in front**, never getting beaten. Ask the kids **WHY?** (If they get behind you, they have a clear path to the basket.) Run this at the main basket for **Cruz and Jackson**. **Part 2:** split into **2 groups, one at each basket**, and play live **1v1, winner stays on**. | Main basket, then both baskets | Stay in front, slide feet, contain the ball |
| 8 min | Help defense shell | **3v3** or **4v4**. Offense **moves the ball** around the perimeter. On every pass, defenders **get into proper help position**: on-ball defender pressures, off-ball defenders sink toward the lane, see man and ball. Start slow, then speed up the passes. | Half court | Move on every pass, help position |
| 7 min | 3-man weave + 2v1 | Run the weave, then go straight into a **2v1**. | Sideline to sideline | Turn the weave into an advantage finish; defend outnumbered |
| 5 min | Round the world | Full court, full speed **around-the-world layups**, 2 teams. | Full court | Cardio, finish at speed |
| 5 min | Corners (whiteboard) | Use the whiteboard. **Where should you go?** Show the **area** to go to, not one spot. **Wait or move?** Which one helps your teammate? Then draw the **screen and roll from the high post**. | Sideline / No basket | Know where to be and when to move |
| 10 min | Half court | **4v4**, one offensive player at the **high post**. **10 passes, then score.** Look for the **screen and roll from the high post**. | Half court | Apply corners and screen-and-roll in live play |
