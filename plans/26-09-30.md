# Wednesday 9/30 — 60 min

Ball movement day. After a quick look back at the last game, we sharpen ball
handling and pass-catch-protect habits, run the **3-man weave into 2v1**, then
build to **hot-potato** passing with screens, first without defense and then live.
The scrimmage rewards passing ahead instead of bunching around the ball.

| Time | Block | Detail | Court | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Roundtable | Circle up: **player names**, talk through the **last game**, and preview today's drills. | Sideline / No basket | Set the tone, build team identity |
| 5 min | Stationary dribbling | **Hard dribble**, **hard dribble cross**, **hard dribble tween**. **Eyes up** throughout, both hands. | Spread out / No basket | Ball control without looking down |
| 5 min | Shooting — 3 shooters at 3 baskets | Each shooter: **pass, catch, protect the ball, shoot**. Rotate after each shot. | 3 baskets | Catch-and-shoot with the ball protected |
| 5 min | Layups — 2 or 3 baskets | **Pass, catch, protect, dribble, 2-hand layup**. Rotate lines. | 2–3 baskets | Clean finish off a catch |
| 5 min | 3-man weave | Sideline to sideline. Pass and go behind. | Sideline to sideline | Passing on the move, filling lanes |
| 5 min | 3-man weave + 2v1 | Run the weave, then go straight into a **2v1**. | Sideline to sideline | Turn the weave into an advantage finish; defend outnumbered |
| 10 min | Hot-potato (no defense) | **3-second** limit per touch. **N-1** players pass and **switch / use off-ball screens**, with a **man in the middle**. **10 passes, no dribbling.** Move players to new spots every 10 passes. | Half court | Quick decisions, move after the pass, keep spacing |
| 10 min | Hot-potato (live) | **3-second** limit. **N-1 vs N**: pass and switch / off-ball screens, man in the middle, **plus defense**. Dribbling allowed; **score after 5 passes**. | Half court | Keep the ball moving under pressure |
| 10 min | Scrimmage | Rest of practice. **Rule:** pass to midcourt; **turnover if you're too close to the ball carrier**. | Full court | Spread out, pass ahead, play the game |
